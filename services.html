SERVICES

Tailored support for individuals, Business Schools and companies

I offer tailored support for individuals, Business Schools and companies that want stronger career outcomes, better positioning and more effective Talent Acquisition practices.

Who I work with

I work with professionals at every career stage, including students and recent graduates, post-master and highly specialised profiles, MBA and Executive MBA participants, middle managers, senior professionals, directors and executives, in Italy and internationally.

1. Career Coaching and Personal Positioning

One-to-one support for professionals who want more clarity, stronger positioning, greater confidence and a more intentional career strategy, including CV and LinkedIn review, interview preparation and career transition support.

2. International Career Development

Support for professionals, students and graduates who want to build, strengthen or accelerate an international career in Italy or abroad.

3. Speaking and Workshops

Sessions for Business Schools, MBA and Executive MBA cohorts, events and companies on Talent Acquisition, career development, personal branding, AI in recruitment and the future of work.

4. Teaching for Business Schools

Teaching availability in Human Resources and Talent Acquisition for Business Schools in Italy and internationally, with a practical, market-oriented and international perspective.

5. Talent Acquisition Training and Consulting for Companies

Training and consulting for companies and Talent Acquisition teams on recruiting procedures, process design, operating models, efficiency and innovation.
