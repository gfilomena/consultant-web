About Me

Alessandro Marangi

International Speaker | Career Coach | Talent Acquisition Expert

Who I Am

I’m Alessandro Marangi, a Career Coach, Talent Acquisition Expert, International Speaker and Lecturer with experience across global organisations including Microsoft, Novartis, McKinsey, General Electric and Johnson & Johnson.

I teach Talent Acquisition at Meliusform Business School and Rome Business School, and I work with professionals at every career stage, from students and recent graduates to middle managers, senior professionals, directors and executives, in Italy and internationally.

I also support companies and Talent Acquisition teams through training and consulting on recruitment processes, organisational models and innovative hiring practices.

My Approach

My approach is shaped by experience across multinational environments as an HR Business Partner, in Executive Search, and as a Senior Talent Acquisition Expert. I combine career coaching, Talent Acquisition expertise and international market insight to help individuals strengthen their positioning and help organisations make better hiring decisions with more clarity, structure and impact.

Speaking and Teaching Topics

My speaking, teaching and workshop sessions focus on career development, international job markets, innovative CVs, interview preparation, personal branding, Talent Acquisition, recruiting, AI in recruitment and the future of work.

I deliver these topics for Business Schools, MBA and Executive MBA cohorts, companies and Talent Acquisition teams in Italy and internationally.
